Pour glowing nectar. Light every lantern.

Lumen Sort is a calm water sort / color sort puzzle. Pour the glowing liquid until every vial holds one pure colour and its lantern ignites. There are 180 hand-checked levels, a Night Garden that grows as you play, and five extra modes.

We're testing on Google Play before launch. It's free, takes a minute to join, and testers keep an exclusive Founding Lantern reward forever.

What to test and how to tell us

  • Play the first 15–20 levels. Is anything confusing, too easy or too hard?
  • Try a mode from the Modes button (Zen, Lantern Rush, the Weekly Tournament and more).
  • Look around the Shop and Style screens. All purchases are simulated in this test: nothing is ever charged, and there are no real ads.
  • Tell us about crashes, freezes, text that doesn't fit your screen, or sounds that are too loud or quiet.

In the game, open Settings → Send feedback. It opens your email app with your app version and phone model already filled in. You can also write to bohus.ionut@gmail.com, or leave private feedback on the Play Store page.
